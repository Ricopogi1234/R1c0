*About the Collection
The collection is about Eraserheads.
The Eraserheads (sometimes stylized as ƎRASƎRHEADS) are widely regarded as "The Beatles of the Philippines," standing as one of the most influential and successful alternative rock bands in the history of Original Pinoy Music (OPM). Formed in 1989 at the University of the Philippines Diliman, the band completely disrupted the local music landscape in the 1990s by shifting the mainstream focus away from superficial pop toward raw, melodic guitar rock.

The Core Lineup
The band consists of four members who met as college students:
Ely Buendia – Lead vocals, rhythm guitar, and primary songwriter
Marcus Adoro – Lead guitar and backing vocals
Buddy Zabala – Bass guitar and backing vocals
Raimund Marasigan – Drums, percussion, and vocals
EraserHeads Awards
International Accolades
MTV Video Music Awards (1997): Won the highly coveted International Viewer's Choice Award for Asia for their legendary music video "Ang Huling El Bimbo". The band flew to New York City to accept the iconic "Moonman" trophy live on stage.
Major Institutional & Lifetime Honors
UP Gawad Oblation (2024): Conferred the highest distinction by their alma mater, the University of the Philippines. This prestigious medal honors their extraordinary contributions to local culture and the Philippine music scene.

Dangal ng Musikang Pilipino (2012): Awarded the Lifetime Achievement Award at the 25th Awit Awards by the Philippine Association of the Recording Industry (PARI).

MTV Pilipinas Music Awards (2004): Honored with a Lifetime Achievement Award following their official 2002 disbandment.

Esquire Philippines (2022): Named Artists of the Year following the announcement of their record-breaking Huling El Bimbo domestic reunion concert.

Local Industry and Radio Awards
NU Rock Awards
The band dominated the NU 107 Rock Awards, setting a record by winning Album of the Year three consecutive times (1994–1996) for Ultraelectromagneticpop!, Circus, and Cutterpillow. They also secured Artist of the Year (1997) and Listener's Choice honors, culminating in their induction into the NU Rock Awards Hall of Fame in 2003.
Awit Awards & Katha Music Awards
Awit Awards: Secured multiple honors from the Philippine Association of the Recording Industry, including Album of the Year for Cutterpillow (1996) and Sticker Happy (1998).

Katha Music Awards: Recognized with top honors for rock album and song categories.**
